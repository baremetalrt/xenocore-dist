    Credits and licences¶

    All · reference · No code

    The same information ships inside the plugin as THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

    XenoCore's own work¶

    The C++ source, Blueprints, data assets, maps, materials, procedural icons and the synthesized sounds (victory chime, rain, thunder) are XenoCore's own, covered by your Fab licence for XenoCore.

    Content from other sources¶

    These are included for use in your project, in games you build with Unreal Engine, under your Fab licence for XenoCore and the terms of their original sources. They are not for standalone redistribution or resale as assets: do not extract them and sell, share or list them on their own or inside another asset pack. Shipping them inside your game is fine.

    Content Where Source
    Cliffs, boulders, scatter and surfaces, cut down and resized Imported/ElectricDreams Epic Games, Electric Dreams Environment, including Quixel Megascans assets
    Granite rock Environment/GaurPlain/Granite Quixel Megascans
    Stylized grass and flowers, wind parameter collection Imported/StyleHex_Studio StyleHex Studio, free stylized grass kit

    VRoid sample models

    pixiv's sample models may not be redistributed for a fee unedited. The seven XenoCore characters ship edited. If you make your own characters from a VRoid sample, edit them too; see Add a VRoid party member.

    Night sky¶

    The night sky textures in World/Sky come from Unreal Engine's CelestialVault plugin:

    • Milky Way: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851). Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO.
    • Moon: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720).

    Keep these credits in your game's credits if you ship these textures. Use of NASA imagery does not imply NASA endorsement.

    AI-generated voices¶

    The sample party's battle voice lines (about 1,149 sound waves in Audio/Voice) were generated with ElevenLabs text-to-speech from scripts written for XenoCore. The Fab listing is marked Created with AI for this reason. See Battle voices to replace them.
