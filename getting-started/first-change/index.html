    Your first change¶

    Designer · 10 min · No code · Last tested: never

    Result: your party leader has a new name and portrait and starts at a higher level. You'll see it on the HUD and in the Party menu the moment you press Play.

    Before you start¶

    • Tour of the project

    How a party member is put together¶

    Each party member is two assets:

    Asset Example Decides
    Member definition Characters/VRoid/Kael/PartyMember_VRoid_L Name, portrait, battle role, and which pawn Blueprint to spawn
    Pawn Blueprint named in the definition's Member Class Mesh, abilities, stats, starting level, the Arts they can learn

    The definition is who the character is; the pawn Blueprint is the body they fight in. A reserve member has no body in the world at all, only a definition, which is why the name and portrait live there.

    Steps¶

    1. Find your leader¶

    1. Open Characters/Party/BP_XenoCore_Party. It is the party component: it holds the roster.
    2. In Class Defaults, under XenoCore › Party, look at Starting Roster. The members are fielded in this order until Max Active Members is reached; the rest start in reserve. Leader Slot says which of the active members you control, 0 being the first.
    3. Note the leader's entry and open it. In the sample, the roster is two VRoid characters, Kael (PartyMember_VRoid_L, the leader) and Mira (PartyMember_VRoid_K). The other five sample characters join later, recruited from NPCs on Gaur Plain.

    2. Rename them and change their portrait¶

    In the member definition, under XenoCore › Party:

    1. Set Display Name to a name of your own.
    2. Set Portrait to a different texture from Characters/Party/Portraits, for example T_XenoCore_Portrait_Orange.
    3. Save.

    3. Start them at a higher level¶

    1. In the same definition, find Member Class and open the Blueprint it names (the magnifying glass beside the field jumps to it).
    2. Click Class Defaults. Under XenoCore › Combatant, set Combatant Level to 20.
    3. Compile and Save.

    Combatant Level is the level a character starts at. After that the progression system owns their level, and it is saved with the game. Every stat is read from a curve at that level, so HP, Strength, Ether and Agility all rise together.

    Test it¶

    1. Open Maps/L_XenoCore_Gym and press Play.
    2. The leader's HUD plate shows the new name and portrait.
    3. Open the menu (pad Y, keyboard I) and go to the Party screen. The leader reads Lv 20, with higher HP and stats than before.

    Starting from the title screen instead

    Continue on the title screen loads your newest save, and a save remembers each character's level. A changed starting level shows up in a New Game, or in a map started directly with Play, as above.

    If it doesn't work¶

    The member definition shows a red error in the Content Browser

    Member Class is empty, or its pawn has no Combatant Pawn Data. The party cannot spawn that character. Set both.

    The level changed but the stats did not

    The pawn's Stats Effect is empty. The character then runs on default stats, and an error naming the pawn is logged on every Play. Set it back to the stats effect it had.

    You now have¶

    A character changed by you, and the two-asset shape every party member shares.
