    Give an enemy Arts¶

    Designer · 40 min · No code · Last tested: never

    Result: your enemy uses its own Arts, with its own animations and cooldowns, and chains them on the party the way the minion does: a Break, then a Topple once its target is Broken.

    Before you start¶

    • Add an enemy type. You should have your own enemy Blueprint.
    • Create an attack art. Enemy Arts are ordinary Art definitions.
    • Reactions and statuses: Break, Topple, Daze.

    How enemies choose Arts¶

    An enemy fights with the same AI as a companion. Every enemy runs the shared StateTree Characters/Enemies/Core/AI/ST_XenoCore_EnemyFreeroam through the AI controller BP_AIC_XenoCore_Enemy. While it has someone to fight, the tree's Fight state runs three tasks:

    Task Job
    XenoCore Chase Aggro Target Moves into reach of whoever holds the most aggro on it
    XenoCore Fight Target Keeps its auto-attack swinging at that target
    XenoCore Use Best Art Every Decision Interval, scores the Arts on its Arts list and uses the best one that is ready

    So an enemy's Arts are data on the enemy, not states in the tree. The tree never names an Art, and one tree serves an enemy with no Arts and an enemy with ten.

    Scoring is exactly the companions': an Art must be off cooldown and its target in reach, then it scores its AI Priority plus any condition bonuses. See How a companion chooses an art.

    The sample minion, Characters/Enemies/Minion/B_XenoCore_Minion_Melee, has two Arts in Characters/Enemies/Minion/Arts:

    Art Does AI
    DA_XenoCore_Minion_Rend Break +2 priority while the target is not Broken
    DA_XenoCore_Minion_Sweep Topple Held until the target is Broken, then jumps the queue

    So a minion that Breaks a party member follows up with a Topple: the same chain the party runs on it.

    Steps¶

    1. Make the Art¶

    1. Duplicate Characters/Enemies/Minion/Arts/DA_XenoCore_Minion_Rend into MyGame/Enemies/Golem and name it DA_MyGame_Golem_Crush. Or start blank, as in Create an attack art.
    2. Open it and set the usual Art fields: Display Name, Power, Distance (its reach), Cooldown Duration and Effects (for a Break, GE_XenoCore_Reaction_Break).
    3. Give it its own Cooldown Tag, for example Cooldown.MyGame.Art.GolemCrush, from your own tag file.
    4. Set Montage to a montage on your enemy's skeleton, made for this Art.

    Fails silently: an Art that borrows the auto-attack's montage

    Using an Art stops the enemy's auto-attack, and the auto-attack stops its swing by montage. If the Art plays the same montage asset as the auto-attack, it is stopped on its first frame: no hit, no damage, nothing logged. Duplicate the clip's montage under the Art's own name, as the minion's AM_XenoCore_Minion_Art_Rend and _Sweep are.

    Fails silently: two Arts sharing a cooldown tag

    A duplicated Art keeps the original's Cooldown Tag. Two Arts with one tag go on cooldown together: using either one makes both unavailable. Give every Art its own tag.

    2. Teach it when to fire¶

    In the Art's Art › Companion AI section:

    Setting Value
    Usable By AI On. Off, the enemy never picks it
    AI Priority Higher wins. 1 is the default
    AI Conditions Optional. For a Break opener, add Target lacks tag Status.XenoCore.Reaction.Break, not Required, Bonus 2
    AI Wait For Reaction On for a Topple or Daze Art: it is held until its reaction can land
    AI Chain Bonus Extra score while its reaction can land. Default 3

    A Topple or Daze Art needs no conditions: the enemy reads the Art's own Effects and holds it until the target is Broken (or Toppled). See The reaction chain.

    Art › Aggro › Threat sets how much aggro the Art builds when an enemy uses it. It matters only for party Arts; you can leave it.

    3. Give it to the enemy¶

    1. Open B_MyGame_Golem, Class Defaults.
    2. Under XenoCore › Enemy › Arts, add your Art to Arts. Remove the minion's if your enemy was duplicated from it.
    3. Compile and Save.

    Arts on this list are granted at level 1 when the enemy spawns. Enemy Arts do not level: the enemy's own Enemy Level already scales its damage through its stats.

    Nothing goes in the ability set: the list is the whole job.

    4. Pace it¶

    Under XenoCore › Enemy › Arts › AI Profile:

    Setting Default What it does
    Decision Interval 1.5 s Time between Art decisions. The auto-attack keeps swinging in between
    Decision Jitter 0.3 Random spread on each interval, so a group of enemies does not act in unison
    Post-Art Cooldown 3 s After any Art, choose no other for this long, on top of the Art's own cooldown
    Reliability 100 % Chance to act at all on a decision. Lower makes a lazier enemy
    Seek Positional Bonus Off for enemies Leave off: enemies fight from where they stand

    A boss with a long Post-Art Cooldown and high-power Arts feels very different from a swarm enemy with short cooldowns and weak ones, with no AI changes at all.

    Test it¶

    1. Place your enemy in Maps/L_XenoCore_Gym and press Play.
    2. Hit it once, then stand still and let it fight.
    3. It auto-attacks, then uses its Break: the struck character shows BREAK. If it has a Topple Art, it follows up within Break's window.
    4. Type log LogXenoAI Verbose in the console. Every decision the enemy makes prints as <enemy>: decision - <Art>=<score> ..., listing each usable Art and its score.
    5. To try the follow-up alone, Break yourself with XenoCore.Reaction 1; the enemy should use its Topple Art next.

    If it doesn't work¶

    It only ever auto-attacks

    The Art is not on the enemy's Arts list, Usable By AI is off, its Distance is shorter than the distance the enemy fights from, or the Art's montage is the auto-attack's (see step 1). The decision log shows whether the Art is ever considered.

    It uses Break but never Topple

    Topple needs the target Broken when Topple lands. If Break's window is shorter than the time Topple takes to connect, the chain breaks. Use a faster Topple montage, or lengthen the Break on GE_XenoCore_Reaction_Break. That effect is shared, so check what else uses it first.

    It uses the same Art back to back

    The Art has no Cooldown Duration, or no Cooldown Tag. Raise Post-Art Cooldown on the enemy too.
