    Party tactics¶

    Designer · 10 min read · No code · Last tested: 2026-10-04

    Tactics are standing orders for the companions: who they attack, and how they use their arts. The player sets them at any time, in the field, locked on or mid-fight. They stay set until changed, and they are saved with the game. Orders can be given to the whole party or to one member, as in Xenoblade Chronicles X.

    Control Pad Keyboard
    Switch Attack between Focus and At Will for the whole party Tap X Tap F
    Open the Party Tactics list (open while held) Hold X Hold F
    ...move the highlight D-pad Up / Down Up / Down
    ...change the highlighted tactic's value D-pad Left / Right Left / Right
    ...give the orders to the next / previous member RB / LB Tab / Q

    While an NPC's prompt is offering Trade, X is Trade instead. The prompt on screen shows which.

                                    [M] Map
                      [X] Party Tactics     <- always shown
                     Focus · Heal First     <- shown for a few seconds, then fades

            PARTY TACTICS                   <- the list, while X is held
  [LB]      (portrait) Mira      [RB]
  ------------------------------------
  | Attack              < Regroup >
    Stay Back           < PARTY  >
    Prioritise Healing  < PARTY  >
    Arts                < PARTY  >

    Under the minimap's [M] Map, in the same style, the hint [X] Party Tactics is always on screen, so the button is never a secret. The party's orders appear on a card below it for a few seconds and fade, rather than cluttering the HUD - when they change (a tap of X, or closing the list), when a battle starts, and once when the game loads. The card wraps to the minimap's width and only ever breaks between two orders. The battle line over the leader also announces every change, as XC1's group commands do. The list appears only while X is held, sized to its longest row. No text in it scrolls.

    The tactics¶

    Tactic Values Series
    Attack Focus: the leader's target. At Will: each companion picks its own (see below). Regroup: stop attacking and gather by the leader, weapons drawn - the leader fights alone until it is changed XC1 Focus attacks! / Engage at will! / Come to me!; XC3 Focus Attacks and Follow Leader
    Stay Back OFF / ON: hold a distance from the target and fight with what reaches from there - heals, buffs, long arts. With swords only, a member on Stay Back mostly heals and supports XCX Use ranged attacks!
    Prioritise Healing OFF / ON: heal arts become eligible sooner and outscore attack arts Ours; no Xenoblade tactics list has one
    Arts All: every art. No Talent: never the Talent Art, so the gauge stays full. Auto Only: no arts at all, heals included No Talent: ours, XCX's Keep your tension up! is the nearest. Auto Only: XCX Assemble! behaviour

    Attack and Arts are one tactic each with three values because their answers exclude each other: a companion told to follow you cannot also be focusing your target, and one on auto-attacks only is already holding its Talent Art. The default is Attack: Focus, Arts: All, everything else OFF.

    One member's orders¶

    Pressing RB while the list is open moves it from Whole Party to the first companion, shows their portrait, and puts a white ring under them. On a member's list every tactic has one extra value, PARTY: "same as everyone". Stepping a tactic away from PARTY gives that member their own order; stepping it back to PARTY hands it back to the party.

    Changing a tactic for the whole party clears every member's own order for that tactic, so "everyone" always means everyone.

    Who a companion attacks on At Will¶

    Each companion scores every enemy fighting the party and takes the best. Only enemies already in the fight count: as in every Xenoblade, companions never start a battle themselves - and with squads that link aggro, one that did would pull the whole squad onto you. The weights are on the character's AI Profile › Target Choice (party member definition), so a defender and a healer can choose differently:

    Field Default Effect
    Attacking Me Weight 3 Fight whatever is attacking this character
    Attacking Ally Weight 1.5 Peel enemies off the rest of the party. Raise on a defender
    Leader Target Weight 1 Lean toward the player's fight
    Current Target Weight 2 Stick with the current target rather than flip-flop
    Per Ally On Target Weight -1 Each other companion already on an enemy makes it less attractive, which spreads the party
    Distance Penalty Per Metre 0.1 Prefer the nearer enemy
    Target Reconsider Interval 1 s How often the choice is re-made; a dead target is replaced at once

    What you can change¶

    Everything is on the party component, UXenoCorePartyComponent (the controller's component list in the experience), in Party › Tactics:

    Property What it does
    Default Tactics The orders a new game starts with
    Tactic Info Per tactic: its Label and Description, and its Values in order - each with its own label on the list, battle line, word in the orders under the prompt (empty = not shown) and colour - plus a sound
    Tactics List Which tactics the list shows, top to bottom. Leave one out to hide it
    Heal First Threshold / Heal First Bonus How early, and how strongly, Prioritise Healing heals (70%, +3)
    Stay Back Distance How far from its target a Stay Back member holds (750 cm - past every melee art and the auto-attack). Each member holds on its own side of the target, so several spread round it
    Regroup Ends With Battle Turn every Attack: Regroup back to Focus when a fight ends. Off by default: orders stand
    Follow Party Line The battle line when a member goes back to PARTY
    Tactics Panel Class The widget that draws the prompt and the list. Set a Blueprint child of XenoCoreTacticsPanelBase to restyle it

    The panel widget's own Details hold its look: Tactics › Layout (position, minimum width, portrait sizes, fade and slide), Prompt (the hint on or off, the orders card's width, how long it shows and its fade, which moments show it - a change, a battle start, a load - and Always Show Prompt to keep the card up), Text (fonts, their shared Font Family, and every word it prints) and Colour (accent, rim, shadow, header band, value pills, highlight). A child that authors its own widget tree keeps it, and gets On Tactics Refreshed after every change to draw from.

    The glyphs - X on the prompt, LB / RB on the list - are the rows PartyTactics, TacticsScopePrev and TacticsScopeNext in DT_XenoCore_Actions (the panel's Action Table, drawn with its Glyph Class, Lyra's InputActionWidget). Glyph Class, Action Table and Font Family are soft defaults on the class: they load when the panel is first built, and a Blueprint child can point them elsewhere. Tools/UIAuthoring/party_tactics_build.py writes them from the keys those input actions are really bound to, so re-run it after remapping.

    The buttons are Blueprint children of XenoCoreGameplayAbility_PartyCommand in /XenoCore/Input/Abilities (GA_XenoCore_PartyTacticFocus, ..._PartyTacticsPanel, ..._TacticsScopeNext/Prev). The tap's Tactic, Value A and Value B say what it flips between; to put another tactic on its own button, duplicate it, change those, and map an input to it.

    Fails silently

    A tactic that is not in Tactics List cannot be changed from the list, and nothing says so. It still works if a button or a Blueprint sets it (Set Tactic on the party component).

    If companions stand around while you fight

    Hold X and look at Attack. On Regroup they are doing what they were told - set it back to Focus. Orders are saved with the game, so a Regroup set once comes back with the save.

    Testing¶

    Tools/UIAuthoring/companion_tactics_pie.py sets every value in turn during a real fight and checks what the companions DO: who they target, whether they swing, how far from the leader they stand, which arts they use and whether a hurt ally is healed. Run it after changing tactics, the companion AI or an art's AI settings.

    Saving¶

    Tactics are saved by name - "Attack: AtWill", never a number - so tactics and values can be reordered or added without breaking a save. Each member's record holds only the orders they do not take from the party. A save made before tactics existed loads Default Tactics.

    For Blueprint¶

    Get Tactic Value, Get Attack Order, Is Tactic On, Set Tactic, Toggle Tactic, Clear Member Tactic and Clear Member Tactics on the party component take an optional member definition (empty = the whole party). Every change is broadcast on XenoCore.Party.Message.TacticChanged.
